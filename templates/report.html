Suitability Assessment

Suitability depends on the intended use, local zoning rules, access roads, utilities, soil conditions and legal status.


  • Verify that the land's permitted use matches your purpose.
  • Check road access, water supply, electricity and drainage.
  • Review nearby facilities and transport connectivity.
  • Confirm title documents and any restrictions before purchase.

Investment Risk Checklist

  • Ownership or title disputes
  • Unverified market price or unclear property boundaries
  • Flooding, environmental or access-related concerns
  • Local planning restrictions and future development limits
